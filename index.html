Border Desk

Add to Home Screen to play

On iPhone and iPad, Border Desk runs as an app from your Home Screen. It takes about 20 seconds.

    Veldmark · Eastern Checkpoint

    Border
    Desk

    Check the papers. Compare them with today's rules. Approve, deny, or detain the crooks.

    Story

    10 days

    Your first ten days at the checkpoint. Regulars, a reporter, a smuggler with a fat wallet, and choices that decide how it ends.

    Race

    2–8 players

    Everyone gets the same day and the same travelers. Fastest time wins, and every mistake adds 10 seconds.

    Arcade

    3-minute shifts

    Every day adds a rule, up to day 14. Build a streak for a score multiplier. Finish a shift to unlock the next day.

    Endless

    Best: 0

    Every rule at once, and it only gets harder. Three mistakes and you're done.
